# Deslop language

Edit text to remove recurring AI writing patterns. Preserve supported claims, evidence, technical meaning, and intended tone. Use the examples to recognize patterns, not as a banned-word list.

## Patterns to fix

- **Superficial -ing phrases.** Watch for trailing clauses built on "highlighting", "ensuring", "reflecting", "showcasing", or "fostering". Cut the clause if it only restates or praises the previous point.
- **Vague attribution.** Replace "experts believe", "industry reports suggest", or "some critics argue" with a named source. Without a source, identify the claim as unverified or remove it. Never invent a source.
- **Stock AI vocabulary.** Watch for "additionally", "crucial", "delve", "enduring", "enhance", "garner", "interplay", "intricate", "landscape", "pivotal", "showcase", "tapestry", "testament", "underscore", and "vibrant" when they add ornament rather than meaning. Use a plainer word.
- **Fancy ways to say "is".** Replace "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", or "features" with "is" or "has" when the meaning is unchanged.
- **Formulaic structures.** Watch for forced groups of three, false ranges, "not just X, but Y", and repeated em dash asides. Rewrite when the pattern makes the prose unnatural or misleading.
- **Synonym cycling.** Repeat one clear term instead of rotating through variants such as "protagonist", "main character", "central figure", and "hero".
- **Filler.** Replace "in order to" with "to" and "due to the fact that" with "because". Remove "it is important to note that", "I hope this helps", "let me know if", "of course", and "certainly" when they add no information.
- **Chatbot reactions.** Remove phrases such as "Great question", "You're absolutely right", and "Found the smoking gun". Address the substance directly.
- **Empty claims and generic conclusions.** Replace "robust", "seamless", "SQL you can read", or "the future looks bright" with a concrete fact or plan. Remove the claim if there is nothing specific to say.
- **Vague intensifiers and stacked hedges.** Replace "significantly improves" with a measured result when available. Reduce "could potentially possibly be argued that it might" to the uncertainty supported by the evidence.
- **Abstract metaphors.** Watch for "substrate", "wedge", "vector", "locus", "vantage", "nexus", "primitive", "harness", "surface", "bedrock", "scaffolding", "modality", "paradigm", "gold-plating", "ratchet", "endgame", "north star", and "flywheel" when they stand in for a simpler idea. State that idea directly. Keep established technical meanings.
- **Mannered prose.** Watch for aphorisms such as "wire it or delete it", personification such as "the plan holds it", figurative verbs such as "rides along" or "stands on", and stock phrases such as "a dial worth turning". State the action or fact directly.
- **Decorative formatting.** Remove bold from proper nouns and acronyms unless it carries meaning. Rewrite inline-header lists such as "**Performance:** Performance improved..." when the label repeats the sentence. Remove decorative emojis.

After editing, check that you have not changed supported claims, added facts, or removed justified uncertainty.
