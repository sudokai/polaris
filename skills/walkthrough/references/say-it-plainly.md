# Say it plainly

Rewrite the text the user points to. If they give no pointers, rewrite your last message. 

Rewrite the text more simply and concisely, like one human talking to another, and keep it coherent. Use plain language, but keep it clear and precise. Do not use the dense, hyper-technical AI dialect filled with mannered prose and compound metaphors that LLMs often use. 

Aim for more clarity, less jargon, fewer odd turns of phrase, fewer low-value details, and slightly shorter answers, without losing substance.
