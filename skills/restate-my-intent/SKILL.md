# Restate my intent

Use the conversation to briefly explain, in your own words:

- What the user wants to achieve.
- What problem they are trying to solve and why it matters.
- Any constraints that materially shape the goal.

Use plain language. Distinguish the desired outcome from any proposed solution. Mark uncertain interpretations as assumptions; do not invent motives or requirements. If essential context is missing, say what is unclear.

Keep the response short and focused on understanding. Do not add a plan or start implementing unless the user also asks for that.
